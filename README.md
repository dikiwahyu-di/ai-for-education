# AI for Education

### Exploring Artificial Intelligence for Better Learning Experiences

Welcome to AI for Education, a personal learning and research repository focused on exploring how Artificial Intelligence can transform teaching, learning, and educational practices.

## Objectives

This repository aims to:

- Explore practical applications of Generative AI in education.
- Develop effective AI prompts for teachers and educators.
- Investigate AI-powered instructional design.
- Explore personalized and adaptive learning experiences.
- Document experiments and research ideas in Educational Technology.
